# Brief (identical for both arms)

Build a single-page landing page (one self-contained `index.html`, inline CSS, vanilla JS only if needed, no build step, no frameworks) in **Spanish** for **Gatopardo Media**, a photo & video production studio in El Salvador.

- Clients/audience: hotel and restaurant directors, property developers, architects, premium vacation-rental owners.
- Offer: commercial photography and cinematic video (hotels, gastronomy, architecture/real estate), sold as a business result (more direct bookings / higher perceived value), not as "pretty pictures".
- Goal of the page: get a qualified prospect to click "Solicitar propuesta".
- Required sections: hero; what we do (3 service lines); selected work (portfolio grid — no real photos are available, so design placeholders that still feel intentional, do NOT use stock image URLs); how we work (process); a short proof/credibility block; final CTA with a simple form (name, company, what you need); footer.
- Must be responsive (desktop 1440 and mobile 390) and accessible.
- Constraints: no external images. Web fonts via Google Fonts `<link>` are allowed. Write all copy yourself (Spanish, no lorem ipsum, don't invent client names or fake statistics).
- Output file path is given separately. Do not open or read other arms' folders.
